General physical training

The trainee should warm up with jumping rope or jumping exercise (block A), do 4 strength exercises 10 times each (block B), do 6 special strength exercises 10 times each (block C), do 6 static exercises 1 minute each (block D), and do stretching (block E).

Over time, for example, after a week, one can increase the load, do a 2-minute warm-up, 20 repetitions of strength and special exercises, and 1:30 minutes of static exercises. It depends on one's adaptation.

TRAINING PLAN

  • A
  • B
  • C
  • D
  • E

A. The workout begins with a light jumping exercise. Duration: 1 minute: jumping rope or parsley. One can alternate every other day, or just do parsley if there is no jump rope.

Jumping rope

  • Steady Pace
  • Do a 150 jumps per minute.

Parsley

  • Try to make your movements more sweeping and intense

B. A set of four strength exercises, each performed 10 times:

  1. Push-ups on fists x10
  2. Lifting the torso from a lying position x10
  3. Squats x10
  4. Back deflection x10

Push-ups on fists

  • Hands clenched tightly into fists, stand on the index and middle fingers
  • Elbows along the body to the back, not to the sides
  • The body should remain in a straight line throughout the entire exercise

Lifting the torso from a lying position

  • Hands locked behind head
  • Twisting the body until the elbows touch the knees
  • Feet do not move

Squats

  • Hands locked behind head
  • Straight back
  • Feet firmly on the ground

Back deflection

  • Raise arms and legs as high as possible
  • Smooth raise - smooth descent
  • Legs do not bend at the knees

C. A set of six special exercises, do each 10 times:

  1. Lunges x10 on each leg
  2. Superman x10 on each side
  3. Jumping forward and upward x10
  4. Jumping cross-move on one leg x10 for each leg
  5. Jumping with knees raised to chest x10
  6. Burpy х10

Lunges

  • Alternate legs: left and right lunges are one repetition.
  • The back knee almost touches the floor
  • Straight back

Superman

  • Alternate sides: left and right lifts are one repetition.
  • The opposite arm and leg are raised smoothly as high as possible

Jumping forward and upward

  • From a squatting position, jump as far up and forward as possible.
  • Landing on the whole foot maintaining balance
  • Try to jump as far as possible with each repetition
  • Do not touch the floor with your hands, especially when landing.

Jumping cross-move on one leg

  • Maintain balance without putting your raised foot down on the ground
  • Jump sideways, sideways, forward, backward, back to the starting position — that's one repetition.
  • Do not lower your second leg during the entire exercise, and do not hold on with your hand

Jumping with knees raised to chest

  • Jump as high as possible, pulling your knees up as close to your chest as possible

Burpy

  • Fall to the ground, touching your chest, pull your knees up to your chest, jump up, and clap your hands above your head

D. A set of six static exercises, each exercise performed for 1 minute:

  1. Plank 1 minute
  2. Stand on one leg for 1 minute on each leg
  3. Arabesque for 1 minute on each leg
  4. Fiststand for 1 minute
  5. Corner 1 minute
  6. Sumo 1 minute

Plank

  • Stand on your forearms and toes, keeping your body in a straight line.

Stand on one leg

  • Maintain balance

Arabesque

  • Stand on one leg, move the other leg back, and lean your body forward to form a straight line

Fiststand

  • A properly formed fist rests on the floor with the index and middle fingers
  • The body is a straight line

Corner

  • Knees straight, feet 10-20 cm above the floor

Sumo

  • Back straight, arms behind the head or at the sides, legs double shoulder width apart, bent at a right angle

E. The workout ends with stretching: butterfly, bends, splits.

Stretching

  • Butterfly, bends, splits on the exhale.
